# Thousand-star growth experiment

Patchline runs a thousand-star growth experiment with measured **funnel** metrics and reproducible interventions.

## How it works

The worker checks each growth intervention has measured funnel metrics and is reproducible.

## What the gate proves

- Every intervention is measured and reproducible.
- An unmeasured intervention is rejected.

## Why it matters

Measured, reproducible interventions turn star growth into an experiment instead of a guess.

## Reproduce

```
make thousand-star-growth-gate
```
