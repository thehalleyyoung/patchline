# Research-replication leaderboard

Patchline runs a research-replication **leaderboard** inviting external teams to beat the published numbers.

## How it works

The worker checks each leaderboard entry reproduced the protocol and scored at or above the baseline.

## What the gate proves

- Every entry is reproduced and meets the baseline.
- An unreproduced entry is rejected.

## Why it matters

An open leaderboard turns the published numbers into a standing, contestable challenge.

## Reproduce

```
make replication-leaderboard-gate
```
