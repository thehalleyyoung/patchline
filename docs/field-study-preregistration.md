# Pre-registered field study with control group

Patchline runs a **pre-registered**, adequately-powered field study with real teams and a control arm.

## How it works

The worker checks every study arm carries a pre-registration and meets the target statistical power.

## What the gate proves

- Every arm is pre-registered and adequately powered.
- An unregistered or underpowered arm is rejected.

## Why it matters

Pre-registration plus power is what separates a credible field result from a fishing expedition.

## Reproduce

```
make field-study-preregistration-gate
```
