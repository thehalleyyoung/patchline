# Five-minute landing flow

Patchline offers a 'protect your migration in 5 minutes' landing flow whose **completion rate** is measured.

## How it works

The worker computes the funnel completion rate from recorded starts and finishes and checks it clears a threshold within the time budget.

## What the gate proves

- The measured completion rate clears the threshold within five minutes.
- A high-friction flow below the threshold is flagged.

## Why it matters

Measuring funnel completion turns onboarding from a guess into something you can actually improve.

## Reproduce

```
make five-minute-landing-gate
```
