# Impact retrospective on design decisions

Patchline ties every major design decision to a measured **outcome** in an impact retrospective.

## How it works

The worker checks each major design decision references a concrete measured outcome.

## What the gate proves

- Every design decision is outcome-backed.
- An unmeasured decision is rejected.

## Why it matters

Tying decisions to measured outcomes turns the retrospective into evidence of disciplined design.

## Reproduce

```
make impact-retrospective-gate
```
