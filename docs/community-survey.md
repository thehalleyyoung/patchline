# Annual community survey driving the roadmap

Patchline runs an annual community **survey** with published results driving the roadmap.

## How it works

The worker checks each survey cycle published its results and informed the roadmap.

## What the gate proves

- Every cycle is published and roadmap-driving.
- An unpublished cycle is rejected.

## Why it matters

A published survey that visibly drives the roadmap keeps the community invested in the project.

## Reproduce

```
make community-survey-gate
```
