# Signed adoption case-study series

Patchline assembles an adoption case-study series with **signed** bundles from at least ten organizations.

## How it works

The worker checks each organization's case-study bundle carries a valid signature.

## What the gate proves

- Every case study bundle is signed.
- An unsigned bundle is rejected.

## Why it matters

Signed bundles from ten organizations make the adoption story verifiable, not anecdotal.

## Reproduce

```
make adoption-case-studies-signed-gate
```
