# Best-paper-readiness self-assessment

Patchline scores a best-paper-readiness self-assessment against published award **rubric**s with evidence.

## How it works

The worker checks each rubric criterion is scored and references backing evidence.

## What the gate proves

- Every rubric criterion is scored with evidence.
- An unsupported criterion is rejected.

## Why it matters

Scoring against real award rubrics with evidence turns 'best-paper-ready' into a defensible claim.

## Reproduce

```
make best-paper-readiness-gate
```
