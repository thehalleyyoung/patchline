# Public roadmap with gate-backed burndowns

Patchline maintains a public roadmap with quarterly, gate-backed milestone **burndown**s and retrospectives.

## How it works

The worker checks each quarter's burndown is gate-backed and has a retrospective.

## What the gate proves

- Every quarter is gate-backed with a retrospective.
- An unbacked quarter is rejected.

## Why it matters

Gate-backed burndowns with retrospectives make the roadmap a record of evidence, not promises.

## Reproduce

```
make public-roadmap-burndown-gate
```
